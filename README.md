# Rohit Sahu 

AI Research | Scientific Machine Learning | Time-Series Modeling | Deep Learning  

I am a Computer Science undergraduate working at the intersection of machine learning, deep learning, and data-driven scientific modeling. My research experience spans lithium-ion battery degradation and cycle-life prediction, global rainfall forecasting, and time-series learning.

I am interested in understanding how learning systems can extract meaningful patterns from complex data, generalize across conditions, and support reliable predictions in real-world scientific settings.
